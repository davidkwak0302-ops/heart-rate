For a good reading
  • Face the camera. The outline follows your face, and the orange boxes should sit on your forehead and cheeks.
  • Sit still, with bright, even light in front of you (a window or lamp).
  • The first number appears after about 5 seconds and updates every second.
  • Keep this tab visible — browsers slow the camera down in background tabs.

This is an experiment, not a medical device. Don't use it for health decisions.
